Sister Dash - higher jumps

Extract this whole folder, then open index.html to play.

Hold Space, Up Arrow or W to reach the high boxes. A quick tap still gives
a short hop. Jump toward a raised box from the side to land on its top;
jumping directly underneath it opens the box first.

All four characters now jump higher. A fully held jump rises approximately:
  Older Sister: 169 pixels (5.3 tiles)
  Younger Sister: 186 pixels (5.8 tiles)
  Dad: 160 pixels (5.0 tiles)
  Mom: 172 pixels (5.4 tiles)

The raised carrot/candy boxes are 128 pixels (4 tiles) above their platforms.
Small and big forms have the same jump height.

Change: js/player.js, PHYS.JUMP_V increased from 700 to 860 pixels/second.

Verification: the actual player, collision, box and pickup logic was simulated
at the game's fixed 60 Hz update rate. All 29 land-based carrot/candy boxes
passed with every character in both sizes (232 combinations), including
opening the box, collecting the item and landing on top. Short hops, coyote
time, buffered jumps and drop-through controls also passed. Underwater
movement produced identical trajectories to the original game. These are
automated physics checks, not a complete browser playthrough.
