SPEC(1) COMPARED WITH SPEC(2)

Spec(1) expands the game from seven to ten levels. New artwork in this pack:

Level 4: TREETOP HEIGHTS
Boss: Drumbeak, the red-crested woodpecker.
Enemies: squirrel, caterpillar, bee.
Scenery/props: giant trees, treehouses, leaf platforms with bending states,
vines, rope bridges, pinecones, bee hives, branch ladder, nest, trunk holes.
Boss effects: bark chips, falling leaves, dive target and bouncing acorn.

Level 8: STARLIGHT STATION
Boss: Fuzzmo, the original lime-green and teal fluffy space creature.
Enemies: purple blob alien, mini UFO, space urchin.
Scenery/props: stars, planets, Earth, moon, space station, rocket, glass dome,
asteroids, metal platforms, laser off/warn/on states, boost pad, meteors,
meteor target, gravity well and space-walk boundary marker.
Boss effects: static sparks, sneeze star, boing wave, victory fuzzball/saucer.
Shared round glass helmet overlays are supplied for all four characters;
scale and position them over the animated heads, including big/small forms.

Level 10: CEMENT CITY
Boss: Brutus Block, the grumpy cracked cement block.
Enemies: pigeon, hopping traffic cone, shopping cart; pebble-friend minion.
Traffic: car, bus and cement mixer truck, each with four animation frames.
Scenery/props: skyscrapers, cranes, steel girders, construction fences,
pavement, roads, crosswalk, pedestrian signal red/green/dim states,
wet cement, falling cement bags, manhole/steam, rooftop air conditioner.
Boss effects: rubble, cement splash, cracked floor and tiny victory brick.
UI: three new world badges, skull-and-skyscraper difficulty badge, boss
portraits, pedestrian indicators, target and vine-grab icon.

CHANGED CHARACTER
Spec(1) describes the younger sister with blonde pigtails, pink T-shirt and
black pants. Spec(2) describes loose curls, a purple hoodie and olive cargo
trousers. This pack supplies a separate pink-outfit variant, small and big,
with idle/run/jump/duck/swim/cast/hurt/victory poses plus two vine poses.
The existing purple design is not overwritten.
